About IronBuddy: Workout Tracker

IronBuddy: Workout Tracker is an Android app for planning and logging gym workouts. It is designed around one idea: your training data belongs to you.

What IronBuddy does

IronBuddy helps you log sets, reps, weight, and notes for every workout, follow a training split, chart training volume and 1RM (one-rep max), calculated with the Epley formula, and track body weight and body fat percentage. An exercise library of more than 800 exercises with demonstrations helps you pick the right movement, and it all works offline.

See the full feature tour with screenshots.

How IronBuddy handles your data

Your workouts, body metrics, notes, and settings are stored on your device. IronBuddy has no account system and does not operate a cloud sync service, so your training data is not sent to IronBuddy servers. Copies can exist only in ways you control or Android provides: Android Auto Backup if you have Google backup turned on and, in the full version, an automatic backup file and files you export or share.

Read the plain-language overview, a privacy-focused fitness app, or the complete privacy policy.

How IronBuddy is priced

IronBuddy has no ads and no subscriptions. It is free to download with a free trial, and after the trial a one-time in-app purchase through Google Play is required to keep logging and editing after the trial. Details are on the no subscription, no ads page.

Health and fitness notice

IronBuddy is a fitness logging and planning tool. It is not a medical device and does not diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any medical condition. Progress charts and estimated strength figures are informational calculations based on the data you enter, not clinical advice. Consult a qualified healthcare professional for medical advice.

Contact and follow

For support or privacy questions, use the contact page or write to iron.buddy.app@gmail.com. Please do not send passwords or unnecessary personal documents.
